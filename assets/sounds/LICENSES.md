# Sound library: sources and licences

Every recording used by the show's sound design, where it came from and under which licence. All sources are **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**: free for commercial use, livestreams and recorded clips, no attribution required (credit is given here anyway). Nothing here is ripped film or game audio; nothing was bought.

- **Freesound** (freesound.org): CC0-only searches; each sound page's licence link was checked before downloading. Freesound's original uploads need a login, so the files are its high-quality OGG previews (lossy, about 192 kbps Vorbis), kept unmodified in `_src/freesound/`. Every candidate was measured and inspected as a spectrogram first (clipping, noise floor, traffic, voices and birds in the background); rejected candidates are listed in SOUND.md.
- **Kenney** (kenney.nl) packs *RPG Audio* and *Impact Sounds*: CC0, original OGG files, kept unmodified in `_src/kenney_*/` with the pack's License.txt.
- **Created for this show**: his nonverbal sounds rendered with his own TTS voice (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0 model, the voices the show already uses) by `scripts/sound/render-voice.mjs` (`_src/voice/`); the charging whines and the short bolt buzz are synthesized in `scripts/sound/build.mjs`; reverbs are generated impulses.

Runtime files (`<category>/*.ogg`, Ogg Opus) are rebuilt from these sources by `node scripts/sound/build.mjs`; `manifest.json` lists each sound's variants with their measured loudness and true peak.

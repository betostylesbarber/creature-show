Go live

Start your LIVE on TikTok first, then press the button. Chat and gifts are only read, nothing is posted.

Add the stream window to OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio

  1. OBS (best quality): Sources → + → Browser. Paste the address, set width 1080 and height 1920, tick Control audio via OBS. Stream to TikTok with your stream key, or start OBS's Virtual Camera and pick it as a camera in TikTok LIVE Studio.
  2. TikTok LIVE Studio only: open the stream window in Chrome, make it tall, and add it with Window Capture. Turn on desktop audio so his voice is heard.
  3. Keep the AI label on screen: TikTok requires AI-generated content to be labelled.

Graphics

Each profile renders a fixed frame size whatever the window is: Low and Medium 1080×1920 (Low without shadows or glow), High 1620×2880, Ultra 2160×3840. TikTok LIVE delivers at most 1080p. The free local AI shares the graphics card with the scene: measured on an RTX 3060, it writes about twice as fast on Medium at 30 fps as on High at 60 fps (38 against 18 text pieces a second), so Medium at 30 fps gives the quickest replies. Use High or Ultra with the Claude brain, or for recordings. The stream window's live fps shows below while it is open.

Layout and safe areas

These zones are estimates, not official TikTok safe areas. TikTok's LIVE screen changes between app versions, phones and views, so check the layout in TikTok LIVE Studio's LIVE and For You previews (and on a real phone if you can), then adjust with the Custom profile.

The layout check draws labelled boxes over the stream window and the preview: the estimated TikTok zones (red hatching) and the protected regions for the face, hand gestures, captions and supporter information. It is off unless you turn it on here, and switches itself off after 20 minutes. Turn it off before you go live.

Audience moments

Short, optional moments that make the stream feel alive. They only start in a quiet spell (no comments waiting, nothing being said, not right after he answered someone or a gift arrived), are free to join, never ask for gifts, and "Unstick" clears them.

Background music

    They loop in order and duck under his voice. Only use music you have the rights to stream.

    Halloween events

    Five events, each started by one verified gift value. Each single gift or completed combo is judged on its own value; gifts never add up. A gift under the first value gets a short thank-you and no event. The values are coins; each must stay above the one before it.
